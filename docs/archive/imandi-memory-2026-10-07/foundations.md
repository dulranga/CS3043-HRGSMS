| Billing policy — 25 September 2026 | SRS §4.7.4/§6.1.4 fixes one base-rate snapshot per room line, reserved-night charges, no automatic early-departure discount, a single DRAFT→FINAL invoice per booking with a required FK to an immutable typed `billing_policy` version, ordered/rounded adjustments, and positive PAYMENT/REFUND records.
