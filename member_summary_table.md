dulranga (Member 1) handles authentication and guest profiles. They build the login/logout session system with hashed passwords, manage staff accounts with roles and branch assignments, and maintain guest profiles while protecting identity references. They also create audit triggers and review audit trails.

imandi (Member 2) manages room inventory and reservations. They maintain branches, room types, amenities, rooms, and blocks, enforce no-overlap rules for bookings, and build availability search and booking creation/modification. They preserve rate snapshots and handle booking status changes.

kulunu (Member 3) handles check-in, active stays, and chargeable services. They implement atomic check-in/check-out procedures, record service usage with frozen price snapshots, and support service reversals while retaining original records. Service recording is only allowed for checked-in bookings.

chamikara (Member 4) manages billing, payments, checkout, cancellation, and no-show. They generate invoices with immutable line items, record partial payments, enforce zero-balance checkout, and handle cancellation with fee tracking and no-show transitions. All financial actions are audited.

thusath (Member 5) handles management reports, administration, audit review, and system configuration. They create the five mandatory SQL reporting views, build report screens with CSV export, manage user/config updates through audited transactions, and provide read-only audit review interfaces.
