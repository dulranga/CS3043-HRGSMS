## Contracts that must be agreed before dependent migrations

1. All original ER ID PKs and matching FKs are `UUIDv7` except `system_config.config_key varchar(255)` and the composite `room_type_amenity` key; the target-only `billing_policy.billing_policy_id` and invoice policy FK are UUIDv7/`uuid` too. The working contract is PostgreSQL 18 `uuid DEFAULT uuidv7()` with version checks on PKs; test it on every owner table and avoid mixed UUID/BIGINT migrations (TBD-09).
2. The original ER had one booking-level date/rate/status set and `room.booking_id`. The target has one `booking` header with one or more separately dated/priced `booking_room_line` rows, line status/revision histories and `booking_room_assignment` as the sole stored physical-room link. It removes `room.booking_id`, `booking.room_id` remains absent, and current occupancy derives from an open assignment joined to a CHECKED_IN line. Physical room condition stores READY/CLEANING/OUT_OF_SERVICE only. Members 2–4 review locking, partial line transitions and corrective migrations; Member 5 consumes the line/derived occupancy contract. Record these as explicit ER/SRS amendments, not original ER attributes.
3. `enum(n)` is an ER annotation, not a `varchar(n)` length or required label count. Member 2's booking-channel, target room-line status and physical room-condition sets are in M2-S01; invoice status is DRAFT/FINAL and payment kind PAYMENT/REFUND. SRS §6.1.4 now proposes payment status/method, invoice-line type and audit-action labels; Members 1/4 must approve/test those domains before dependent DDL (TBD-07).
4. Member 2 rates map to non-negative LKR `numeric(12,2)` with PostgreSQL rounding ties away from zero. SRS §6.1.4 proposes exact service quantity/price, invoice/payment amount, `nic` and `text(65535)` mappings for owner review. ER event `timestamp` maps to UTC `timestamptz` with Asia/Colombo display (TBD-08/TBD-10).
5. Actor FKs `created_by`, `changed_by`, `recorded_by`, `voided_by` and `updated_by` target `user_account.user_id`, with authenticated users for user actions and a non-login system principal for jobs. Member 1 must implement and test that shared contract; not-yet-occurred event fields remain nullable (TBD-11).
6. The approved stack is README's React 18/TypeScript/Vite frontend and Express/TypeScript/Node API with PostgreSQL and raw parameterized SQL. New JSON routes use `/api/*`; existing `GET /rooms` remains a compatibility alias during caller migration (TBD-12).
7. Online guest accounts are in scope: `guest_account` links `user_account` to `guest`; online guests may access only their own profile/bookings, search availability, create direct bookings and request eligible cancellation. Staff-assisted bookings for guests without accounts remain in scope. Verify contact/identity control before account linking; never trust a submitted guest ID as proof of ownership.
8. SRS §4.7.4/§6.1.4 is the Version 1 billing contract: server-confirmed fixed base-rate snapshots per room line, reserved-night charging, one DRAFT→FINAL invoice per booking with an immutable FK to Member 1's effective chain-wide typed `billing_policy`, ordered two-decimal discount/service-charge/tax/flat-fee lines, and positive typed PAYMENT/REFUND records. A later-published same-date policy row corrects an error for future confirmations without changing existing invoice FKs. Every line checkout needs exactly zero consolidated balance; partial checkout remains DRAFT. A credit is refunded manually before finalization. Demo policy has zero percentages/fees and one no-show grace day; production values require approval (TBD-03). Generic `system_config` is non-financial current-value storage; SRS proposes a controlled server role-permission matrix for Member 1/5 review (TBD-15).
9. Monthly billed revenue is grouped by booking branch and Asia/Colombo month of FINAL `invoice.issued_at`; all signed FINAL lines reconcile once, with room/service/other columns. A multi-month stay is not apportioned across nights; payments/refunds are separate cash movements (FR-070/AT-22).
10. `room_type`, `amenity` and `service` are chain-wide catalogues without a branch FK. The SRS working permission matrix reserves catalogue/price writes for CHAIN_MANAGER; BRANCH_MANAGER edits only own-branch physical rooms/dated blocks, SERVICE_STAFF and FRONT_DESK record own-branch usage but cannot edit service prices, and SYSTEM_ADMINISTRATOR manages branch/account/non-financial configuration. Member 1 and consumers must review/test this mapping under TBD-15 (AT-24).
11. A limited outage is a dated `room_block`; an extended outage is physical OUT_OF_SERVICE. A new/edited block that overlaps a current BOOKED/CHECKED_IN assignment, or room/branch/room-type deactivation or room OUT_OF_SERVICE while affected current assignments remain, is rejected until those assignments are validly closed; a branch's booking cannot simply move across branches. Availability and new bookings require active room, branch and room type. Member 2's M2-S06 guards both sides under concurrency with Member 1's branch API; M2-S08 surfaces affected room lines (AT-23/AT-25).
12. Legacy `RESERVED` is not automatically a dated manual hold. M2-S23 validates each such room against a provable active booking assignment or an explicit dated block; assignment-backed reservations need no invented block, and unexplained/contradictory rows stop migration for reviewed repair (AT-17).
13. Without an explicit approved override, rooms of the same type confirmed together snapshot the same base rate; distinct type rates are tested with a Single+Double booking, while two Single rooms test multiple same-type lines (AT-01/AT-26). M2-S28 prevents a later type-capacity reduction from undersizing a current BOOKED/CHECKED_IN line and prohibits changing a booked room's type at all, including under concurrent writes (AT-27).

## Member 1 — identity, staff, guests and shared foundations

- Establish the common UUIDv7 PK/FK generation and migration contract. Create `branch`, `role`, `user_account` and `officer`; `officer.officer_id` is both its PK and an FK to `user_account.user_id`, while `officer.branch_id` and `officer.role_id` carry staff scope. Do **not** put role/branch FKs back on `user_account` without an approved ER change.
- Create `guest` and `guest_account` (`guest_account_id` PK; `guest_id`/`user_id` FKs). Implement staff-assisted guest/profile management plus online account registration/linking, own-profile/history access and account-takeover protection while protecting `guest.NIC`; review the SRS §6.1.4 working optional/unique `nic`, no-passport-field and one-to-one guest-account proposals with the team before DDL.
- Own `audit_log` and original-ER `system_config` base DDL plus the target-only typed `billing_policy` table. `audit_log.user_id` references `user_account`; `system_config.config_key varchar(255)` remains its PK for audited non-financial current values, with no Version 1 historical lookup. Publish append-only chain-wide policy versions with validated percentage/fee/grace bounds and actor/audit controls for Members 2/4/5; allow a later-published same-date correction for new bookings, retaining earlier rows/FKs. Do not put financial rules in generic text values.
- Deliver login/logout for staff and online guests, staff/guest administration APIs, SYSTEM_ADMINISTRATOR-only branch-record write API, branch authorization against `officer` roles, and guest ownership authorization through `guest_account`. New endpoints use the selected `/api/*` prefix; candidate paths are `/api/auth/*`, `/api/users/*`, `/api/guests/*`, `/api/branches/*`. Member 1's candidate UI routes are `/login`, `/account`, `/guests/*`, while Member 5 owns the `/admin/users/*` and `/admin/branches/*` UI.
- Verify shared-key one-to-one behavior, branch/role authorization, guest links, audit immutability, UUIDv7 types and safe account deactivation before handoff.

M2-S24–S27 add/backfill room lines and histories, rekey assignments, replace the one-open-per-booking index and retire duplicated booking-header fields. M2-S22/S23 remove `room.booking_id`, convert room condition to READY/CLEANING/OUT_OF_SERVICE and preserve legacy evidence; a RESERVED row may be supported by assignment or block evidence. `booking_room_assignment` remains the only stored physical-room link.
- Preserve branch-scoped room numbers, same-branch lines under one booking, positive per-line guest count, valid line/block dates, immutable charged rates, one open assignment per active line and database-enforced same-room interval conflicts. M2-S06 also prevents two CHECKED_IN lines in one room and guards active assignments against conflicting blocks, room/branch/type deactivation and OUT_OF_SERVICE under concurrency; M2-S28 guards later capacity/type edits. Keep line, assignment and physical-condition histories atomic.
- Build CHAIN_MANAGER-only writes for the shared room-type/amenity catalogue, BRANCH_MANAGER own-branch room/block administration, parameterized `fn_available_rooms` for multi-room availability (not a date-dependent ordinary view), staff booking create/modify/list/detail and online guest create/own reads. Confirm booking under the latest effective policy on the Asia/Colombo date, re-quote if it changes and atomically create Member 4's DRAFT invoice with that policy FK. Online requests use Member 1's authenticated guest ownership; staff retains branch scope. Staff control line/date/room modifications; Member 4 owns per-line/whole cancellation and checkout. Candidate endpoints: `/api/room-types`, `/api/amenities`, `/api/rooms/*`, `/api/room-blocks/*`, `/api/availability`, `/api/bookings/*`; candidate routes: `/admin/rooms/*`, `/search`, `/bookings/*`, `/my-bookings`.
- Hand Member 3 a line-to-current-room lookup, Member 4 per-line rate/status/assignment events and Member 5 historical line/occupancy paths. Verify mixed-type rooms with different rates, two same-type rooms with the same base rate, concurrent conflicts, capacity edits, adjacent dates, moves and preserved history.

New ordinary service usage snapshots the current catalogue price in the same transaction and rejects a client-supplied override; preserve original usage rows on reversal. Member 4 M4-S04 may calculate against M3-S04's schema/test fixtures before M3-S09's recording feature is complete; M3-S09 then consumes M4-S05's DRAFT-bill refresh hook.

Own the chain-wide service catalogue with CHAIN_MANAGER-only catalogue/price writes, plus own-branch FRONT_DESK/SERVICE_STAFF usage recording, optional line attribution and price snapshots; reject usage unless the booking has a CHECKED_IN line, and never attribute a booking-wide usage to an arbitrary room. Member 4 owns checkout; Member 3 supplies the audited physical-condition/history operation in M3-S18 for Member 2's API and Member 4's transaction.

- Test price changes against historical usage, void auditability, check-in rollback and room-status history consistency.

## Member 4 — billing, payment and end-of-stay transitions

- Create `invoice`, `invoice_line` and `payment` with UUIDv7 PK/FK types and ER attributes plus the documented target amendments: unique `invoice.booking_id`, required immutable `invoice.billing_policy_id` FK to Member 1's typed version and `payment.kind` PAYMENT/REFUND. The ER has **no** `invoice.total_amount`; derive totals from protected lines unless an explicit amendment approves a snapshot. Assign invoice number/issue time only at FINAL.
- Own one DRAFT→FINAL invoice per booking, line-summed fixed-rate/reserved-night charges, ordered adjustments from its linked policy, partial payments/manual refunds and per-line checkout/cancellation/no-show. No-show cutoff is local midnight after the policy's grace days (one day in demo). Every line checkout requires exactly zero consolidated balance; partial checkout does not finalize. Whole-booking cancellation affects all eligible lines atomically, removing their provisional room-night charges and applying only the linked policy's flat fees. Online guests may request cancellation only for their own eligible line/booking. Coordinate line history/assignment closure with Member 2 and room CLEANING/history with Member 3. Use Member 1's policy/audit interfaces.
- Candidate endpoints: `/api/bookings/:ref/invoice`, `/api/payments`, `/api/bookings/:ref/lines/:lineId/checkout`, `/api/bookings/:ref/lines/:lineId/cancel`, `/api/bookings/:ref/lines/:lineId/noshow`, plus an explicit whole-booking cancellation route; candidate UI routes remain under `/billing/:ref` and `/bookings/:ref`. Validate line ownership under the booking and confirm paths with consumers before implementation.
- Test exact decimal calculations, immutable finalized invoice lines, partial/failed payments, zero-balance enforcement, cancellation fee representation, guest ownership denial and atomic checkout/room/assignment changes.

## Member 5 — reporting, administration and audit review

- Define the five mandatory reports with line-level dates/status/rates and one booking-level invoice/payment/refund set: occupancy, billing summary, service breakdown, monthly revenue and top services (SRS §6.1.10). Availability is separate. Label DRAFT billing as provisional; group all signed FINAL invoice lines by booking branch and Asia/Colombo month of `issued_at`, with reconciling room/service/other columns. Do not spread one invoice across stay months; reconcile cash payments/refunds separately. Use assignment occupancy segments for historical room attribution; derive current occupancy from open CHECKED_IN line assignments. Avoid multiplying invoice/service rows through multi-line joins.
- Own report screens/filters/exports and read-only audit review. Implement separate non-financial `system_config` current-value UI and typed `billing_policy` publication UI against Member 1-owned interfaces, plus user/branch administration and audit review; coordinate writes with Member 1 rather than adding competing DDL.
- Candidate endpoints: `/api/reports/*`, `/api/reports/export/*`, `/api/admin/audit`, `/api/admin/config/*`; candidate routes: `/reports/*`, `/admin/audit`, `/admin/config`.
- Reconcile report totals to independent booking/invoice/payment/service queries, test branch permissions and CSV parity, and verify audit before/after visibility without permitting audit edits.
